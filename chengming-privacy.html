This is the privacy policy for the Chengming mobile app. It is written for Google Play and for the people who use the app. The app’s screens are in Chinese. This page is in English so the store listing can link here.

What this app is

Chengming is a short daily practice: sit-to-stand, then one brief brain exercise, about ten minutes. It is a personal helper. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Counts and effort ratings describe only the set you just finished.

What we collect

We do not collect your data on our servers. There is no account. There is no cloud backup. We do not sell data. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not use Health Connect, HealthKit, or Google Fit.

On your phone only, the app may store:

  • whether you finished or paused a day
  • each completed set (what you did, how many, how hard it felt, the time)
  • your reminder on/off and clock time, if you chose one

These stay in the phone’s local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can copy a plain-language summary of what you did from the history screen. That copy stays on your device unless you yourself paste it somewhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks the system for permission to show one local notification a day. The reminder is scheduled on the device. It is not sent by us from a server. You can leave it off. The app will not prompt you more than once after you first complete a real set.

Network

The app can run without a network connection for today’s ten minutes. It may try a short, optional look-up of local reading notes on your own machine. If that fails, it shows nothing extra. Practice records are not uploaded.

Children

Chengming is for adults, typically fifty and over, and the family members who help them. It is not directed at children.

Retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your records. What is on the phone remains until you clear app data or uninstall. There is no account to delete with us.
